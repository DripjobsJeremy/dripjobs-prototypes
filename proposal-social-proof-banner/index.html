This banner shows on your proposals in the customer portal once Projects Completed, Years in Business, and Google Rating are all filled in. Don’t offer a satisfaction guarantee? Turn off Show Satisfaction Guarantee to hide it from the banner.

Enter your current Google rating from 1.0 to 5.0, using one decimal place (like 4.8). DripJobs doesn’t pull this from Google or check it, so update it here whenever your rating changes.

Shows “100% Satisfaction Guaranteed” on your banner. Turn this off if your business doesn’t offer a guarantee. Your banner still shows without it.
