How to use this prototype

1
Browse Businesses
Click Businesses in the left nav (new in V1, below Contacts). Click any row to open its profile.
2
Explore the profile tabs
Information holds the entity's own fields. Deals, Proposals, Appointments, Invoices, and Payments aggregate records across every associated contact — try Tasks, Notes, Attachments, Email, and Text too.
3
Create a business
Use + New Business on the list page to see the required Legal Name, optional DBA, multi-email/phone, address, and billing address fields.
4
Check the Contacts list
Click Contacts in the left nav. Note the new Business column — click a business pill to jump straight to its profile, or sort by the column to group Business-associated contacts together.
5
Open a Contact record
From the Contacts list, click a name to see the Business association widget: associate, change, or remove — a Contact can only belong to one Business at a time.
6
Try the four creation entry points
The info banner atop Businesses links to Creation Entry Points, which shows the shared Business / Individual selector as it appears in New Lead, New Appointment, New Proposal, and Create Invoice.
💡
Amber flags = open PRD questions
A few interactions still carry an amber "Assumption" flag where the PRD leaves the exact rule open. Hover for the open item it maps to. (Delete and Call Business are now resolved per BR6/BR10 and no longer flagged.)
