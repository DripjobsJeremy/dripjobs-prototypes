Proposals & Proposal Builder

3 updates

New Proposals Created From a Deal Now Default the Project Manager

Oct 26
What changed
A new proposal created from a deal, whether from the three-dot menu or the Command Center, now fills in the deal's Project Manager along with its Salesperson. Previously the Project Manager field was blank. If the deal has no Project Manager, the field stays blank.
Why it matters
Proposals, and the Inbox conversations that come from them, reach the right Project Manager without extra data entry.
Action needed
None

Clearer Drag-and-Drop Indicators in the Proposal Builder

Oct 19
What changed
Dragging an Area or Line Item now highlights only the places it can actually be dropped. The target lights up while your pointer is over it, and a Package shows an add zone. A Line Item no longer shows the Areas indicator, and the indicators clear when you drop or cancel. Where items land is unchanged.
Why it matters
Building a proposal with packages is clearer, with fewer items dropped in the wrong place.
Action needed
None

Countersign Date Now Stays at the Original Acceptance Date

Oct 13
What changed
Fixed an issue where the countersign date on an accepted proposal changed to the current date whenever the proposal was reopened later. It now stays fixed to the original acceptance date on screen, in PDF downloads, and in emails, and existing accepted proposals keep their historical date.
Why it matters
Your signed proposals show the true acceptance date, which matters for paperwork and disputes.
Action needed
None

Customer Portal & Invoicing

2 updates

Request Payment Percent and Amount Now Stay in Sync and Round Correctly

Oct 29
What changed
In the Request Payment window, typing a dollar Amount now updates the Custom % to match, and an Amount calculated from a percentage now rounds to the nearest cent instead of cutting off the extra digits. For example, 35% of $4,647.02 is now $1,626.46. A percent-based request can now be up to one cent higher than before.
Why it matters
The percent and dollar fields always agree, and the amounts you request are exact to the cent.
Action needed
None

New: Default Document Language for the Customer Portal

Oct 22
What changed
Admins can now set a Default Document Language in Company Settings > Customer Portal for the proposals, invoices, and change orders customers open in the portal. You can also set a language on an individual proposal or invoice, and change orders follow their proposal. Customers get a language selector in the portal header, and their own choice always takes priority. An optional "Also apply to Work Orders" setting is off by default. Translation uses Google Translate on portal pages only, so it is machine-generated, downloaded PDFs stay in the original language, and names, addresses, numbers, and money amounts stay exactly as entered.
Why it matters
Customers who read another language can read your proposals and invoices in it without you translating anything.
Action needed
Optional, set a Default Document Language in Company Settings > Customer Portal.

Pipeline & Mobile

7 updates

Moving a Deal Stage in the Command Center Now Saves to the Proposal

Oct 26
What changed
Changing a deal's stage with the Move option in the Command Center now saves to the deal and its proposal, including reopening a Rejected proposal as Draft, matching what drag-and-drop already did. If the save fails you now see an error instead of a false success.
Why it matters
A proposal you reopen from your phone stays reopened.
Action needed
None

Contact Option Toggles Now Have Info Tooltips

Oct 26
What changed
The Options section on the Contact Detail page now has an info icon beside Active Contact, Opt Out of Blasts/Newsletters, Opt Out of Drips, and Opt Out of SMS. Hover, tap, or focus the icon for a short explanation. The toggles themselves work exactly as before.
Why it matters
You and your office staff can see what each setting does before changing it.
Action needed
None

On-Site Estimate Requests No Longer Linger on the Calendar After Scheduling

Oct 26
What changed
Fixed an issue where scheduling an On-Site Estimate from the Command Center for a deal that came from a booking form left the original request on the calendar as a duplicate. The original request is now updated, so only the scheduled appointment shows.
Why it matters
Your calendar shows one entry per estimate, so you don't chase stale requests or risk double-booking.
Action needed
None

Project Manager "Show All Jobs" Toggle Now Applies Everywhere

Oct 22
What changed
With "Show All Jobs For Project Manager Role" turned on, Project Managers now see all company jobs in both the Jobs Pipeline List and Board views, and all job events on the Job Schedule calendar and the needs-scheduling list. With the toggle off, they still see only their own jobs.
Why it matters
Project Managers with the toggle on no longer miss jobs that need scheduling or attention.
Action needed
None

Mobile App Calendar Now Shows Full Holiday Names

Oct 13
What changed
In the mobile app's calendar month view, holidays now show a recognizable name instead of a single letter, and tapping a holiday shows its full name on iOS and Android. The holiday styling and the Holidays toggle are unchanged.
Why it matters
Your crew can tell which holiday falls on a day when planning jobs from a phone.
Action needed
None

Large Contact List Exports Now Work

Oct 5
What changed
Fixed an issue where exporting the full contact list for accounts with very large lists, around 15,000 contacts or more, ended on a generic error with no file. Exports now complete, each eligible contact appears once, and a failure shows a useful error message. Archived and active filtering is unchanged.
Why it matters
Contractors with big customer lists can get their complete data out.
Action needed
None, retry the export if it failed for you before.

Google Address Suggestions Are Back on Contacts, Proposals, and Invoices

Oct 5
What changed
Fixed the Google address suggestion dropdown not appearing when adding or editing addresses on Contacts, Proposals, and Invoices. Selecting a suggestion fills in the address fields, and typing an address manually still works.
Why it matters
Job-site addresses are faster to enter and cleaner, with fewer typos on quotes and invoices.
Action needed
None

Messaging & Notifications

5 updates

Email Menu Badge Now Matches What You Can See in Your Inbox

Oct 26
What changed
For non-admin users such as salespeople, the unread badge on the Email menu now counts only the conversations they are allowed to see in their Inbox. It previously counted unread conversations company-wide. Admins keep the company-wide count.
Why it matters
Your badge matches your Inbox, so you won't see "1 new" with nothing to open.
Action needed
None

Email Blast Previews and Test Sends Now Match the Editor

Oct 19
What changed
Fixed an issue where an email blast preview or test send could leave out images that appear in the editor and hide the message content behind a "View entire message" link. Previews and test sends now show the images and the full message body.
Why it matters
You can trust that a blast preview matches what your customers will receive.
Action needed
None

Inbox Tags Now Show the Deal's Salesperson and Project Manager

Oct 15
What changed
Inbox conversations sent from a deal now show the deal's assigned Salesperson in the row and thread header tag instead of the account owner, and the Project Manager tag now shows whenever one is assigned. Each conversation reflects its own deal, and the tag matches the Salesperson filter.
Why it matters
Your reps can tell at a glance who owns each conversation.
Action needed
None

Verified Texting Companies No Longer See "Text Messages Are Disabled"

Oct 15
What changed
Fixed a mismatch where a company that was approved and verified for texting could still see "Text Messages are disabled" on Integrations and be sent to a verification form from Chat. Check Twilio Status, Integrations, and Chat now use the same verification state, and if messaging is blocked for another reason, the specific reason is shown.
Why it matters
Verified contractors are no longer locked out of texting and Chat.
Action needed
None

Privacy Policy and Terms Links Now Required for New Text Messaging Registrations

Oct 15
What changed
New A2P 10DLC text messaging registrations now require a Privacy Policy URL and a Terms & Conditions URL, both public HTTPS links that are sent to Twilio. Booking forms now show separate Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions links that open in a new tab without losing the form. Existing approved campaigns are not changed.
Why it matters
Your texting registration keeps meeting Twilio's requirements, and booking form visitors can read your legal terms.
Action needed
If you are registering for texting, have your public HTTPS Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions links ready. Existing campaigns need nothing.

Integrations

4 updates

New: Zapier "New Email Received" Trigger

Oct 22
What changed
A new Zapier trigger, "New Email Received", fires once for each customer email that arrives in the DripJobs Inbox, including replies. It sends the customer's name, phone, and email, the assigned Salesperson and Project Manager, the email content, the date received, and the email type (Proposal, Invoice, Drip Reply, or Change Order).
Why it matters
You can automatically alert your staff or log customer replies in the tools you already use.
Action needed
Turn on "Route all email replies to DripJobs" in Company Settings > Email Settings, then add the trigger in Zapier.

Payment Schedule Added to the Zapier "New Invoice Created" Trigger

Oct 22
What changed
The Zapier "New Invoice Created" trigger now includes a Payment Schedule field that lists each scheduled payment in order, with its name, percentage, and dollar amount. Invoices with no schedule return an empty value, and existing Zaps keep working unchanged.
Why it matters
You can send an invoice's payment milestones into your other tools through Zapier.
Action needed
None

New: Google Calendar Icon and One Place to Manage Your Connection in My Profile

Oct 13
What changed
The Google Calendar status widget on Appointments and Job Schedule is now a compact Google icon that takes every user, admin or not, to a Google Calendar section at the top of My Profile. That is where you connect, manage, and disconnect Google Calendar, with new Sync Appointments and Sync Jobs toggles and a Disconnect toggle. Changes apply when you click Save. Sync is one way, DripJobs to Google, and turning it off stops future syncing without removing events already on Google. Admins also see a read-only Google Calendar card in Settings > Integrations that links back to My Profile.
Why it matters
Any user can connect Google Calendar and choose what syncs, without hunting through admin settings.
Action needed
None, go to My Profile > Google Calendar to connect, change what syncs, or disconnect, and click Save.

Zapier Demo Link Updated

Oct 5
What changed
The demo link in the Zapier box under Company Settings > Integrations now opens the current Zapier overview booking page. Your Zapier connection and settings are unchanged.
Why it matters
If you want help with Zapier automations, the link takes you to the right place.
Action needed
None

Settings

2 updates

Substrate Client Labels Now Limited to 48 Characters

Oct 29
What changed
The Client label on a substrate in Production Rates is now limited to 48 characters, with a counter such as 12/48, in substrate settings and on proposals. The full 48 characters now show on the Customer Portal and the proposal PDF. Existing longer labels are not changed and show a "too long" warning, and you can still save other changes to that substrate, but editing the label text requires 48 characters or fewer. Work order PDFs and the crew view may still clip long labels.
Why it matters
Your customers see the full label on proposals, invoices, and change orders.
Action needed
Shorten any existing labels over 48 characters if your customers see them cut off.

Event Banner Toggles Moved to the Calendar Tab

Oct 5
What changed
"Add crew name to event banner" and "Add job total to event banner" have moved from Company Settings > App Settings > Estimate Settings to Company Settings > Calendar. Every company's current on/off choice is kept and banners behave the same. New accounts now have the job total shown by default, and the crew name default stays off.
Why it matters
Calendar display options are now where you would expect to find them.
Action needed
None, look in the Calendar tab if you adjust these toggles.

Upcoming Releases

5 in progress

Direct Thumbtack Integration

QA complete

A direct Thumbtack integration is in the works. Once live, new Thumbtack leads will flow straight into DripJobs as contacts and deals, tagged with Thumbtack as the lead source, and dropped automatically into your Sales Pipeline and drip sequences, no manual entry required.

Business Entity Records

In QA

Commercial accounts will be able to track a Business as its own record, with several Contacts associated to it and one marked Primary. A new Businesses item in the Sales menu leads to a tabbed Business profile that brings together its deals, proposals, change orders, appointments, invoices, and payments in one place, and Business can be chosen when creating a lead, appointment, proposal, or invoice.

Per-Package Discounts

In QA

Every package on a proposal will get its own discount action, flat or percentage, calculated against that package's subtotal and added as a discount line inside the package. Each package's discount stays independent of the others.

Job Costing Design Refresh

In QA

The Job Costing Dashboard, Details, Reports, Employee Timesheets, Employee Payroll, Time Sheet Entries, Misc. Costs, and Tutorials pages are being updated to match the current DripJobs design, with the same section cards, floating label fields, buttons, and tabs you see elsewhere. The Edit action on Materials and Misc. Costs rows will also work the way Delete already does.

Description Only Option When Adding Multiple Items

PR in review

A Description Only checkbox is coming to the Add Multiple Items window on web and mobile, in proposals, packages, and proposal templates, so you can mark items as Description Only while adding them in bulk instead of editing each one afterward. Description Only items add nothing to the proposal total.
