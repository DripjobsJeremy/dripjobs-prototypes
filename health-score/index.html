HealthScore — All Accounts

CS Team · Super Admin View · All tenant accounts, no date restriction · Updated Aug 5, 2026

What’s new in v2.1: Reworked per Slack feedback from Jason, Rick, and Mary: the grid was slow, showed columns nobody scans (Integrations, Add-Ons, etc.) with no clear health-related action to take on them, and Jason pointed out that fewer than 10 fields actually drive the Health score. The 34-column grid is now 10 health-relevant columns (Tenant ID, Company Name, Type, Created, Health, Status, Total Sales, A2P Status, Days Rem., OBCSS) plus a Details link per row. Clicking a row (or its Details link) opens a slide-over panel with everything that used to be a column — Industry/Account Age, Engagement Signals (Active Users, Contacts, Est. Sent, Last Est., Prop. Viewed, Deals 90d), Plan & Add-Ons, Integrations, and Automations — so that data is still fully available, just loaded per-account instead of for all 1,400 rows at once. This directly addresses Mary's use case (CS pulling a full adoption snapshot right before a call) without paying the render cost for every tenant on every page load. Also added a “Health last calculated” note near the table, signaling these scores come from a scheduled batch job rather than live per-request computation (per Rick's precompute concern) — the actual precompute cadence is a backend decision outside this mockup's scope. Removed two redundant columns while in there: $10K (folded into a colored Total Sales cell) and A2P Applied (A2P Status already covers it). All existing filters, including ones for fields that moved into the drawer (Integrations, Add-Ons, Contacts, etc.), are unchanged — filtering doesn't require the column to be rendered. Health scoring logic itself (computeHealth()) was not touched.

Earlier versions: v1.7–v1.9 added Prop. Viewed/Deals (90d) tracking, reorganized filters into the collapsible Search & Segment / Account Data / Integrations accordion, and removed DripJobs' own plan/billing revenue from the page per Jason's separate feedback that total revenue shouldn't show here.
