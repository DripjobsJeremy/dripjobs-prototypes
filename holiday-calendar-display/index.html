Holiday Calendar Display (US/Canada)

Prototype for ticket 86bbc0rvr. Adds a per-user "Holiday Calendar" preference (None / United States / Canada / United States & Canada) that overlays non-editable, visually distinct holiday blocks on the Appointments and Job Schedule calendars. The two calendars below share one preference, so changing it in either place applies identically to both. Default is None — reload this page and both calendars match the current production look from the tickets' screenshots.

⚠️
Scope limits on the holiday data set
To prove indefinite, rule-based resolution (BR-11), this prototype computes real fixed-date holidays (e.g. July 4) and relative-weekday holidays (e.g. "4th Thursday of November") for any year — try navigating the Month view forward a few years. It intentionally excludes lunar/Easter-based holidays (e.g. Good Friday) and province-only Canadian observances, which would need a fuller reference table than this ticket's examples call for. Company timezone handling isn't rebuilt here — dates use the calendar's existing date grid, consistent with a timezone setting that already exists in Company Settings → Calendar, out of scope for this ticket.

Acceptance criteria coverage
How this prototype demonstrates each acceptance criterion from the ticket.
✓Per-user preference with None / United States / Canada / United States & Canada — the "Holiday Calendar" select in either calendar's toolbar.
✓Defaults to None — both calendars load with no holiday blocks until you change the selector.
✓Changing the preference only affects your own view — simulated here as one shared prototype-session value; no other-user state exists to demo against.
✓Correctly labeled, non-editable holiday blocks on Appointments — Appointments tab, Month or 3 Days view, any region selected.
✓Same on Job Schedule — Job Schedule tab, identical control and block styling.
✓"United States & Canada" shows both, with country labeling on same-named holidays that fall on different dates — select "United States & Canada" and compare October vs. November Thanksgiving — each block gets a US/CA tag.
✓Company-timezone-based date determination — not rebuilt here; holiday dates are computed directly against the calendar grid, consistent with the existing Company Settings → Calendar timezone.
✓Resolves indefinitely, including relative-rule holidays — navigate the Month view forward or back several years; Memorial Day, Thanksgiving, etc. recompute correctly every time.
✓Existing jobs/appointments stay schedulable and editable alongside a holiday block — sample events on Aug 10–11, 2026 render next to holiday blocks with no interaction blocked.
✓Holiday blocks are visually distinct from real events — dashed amber hatch pattern vs. solid purple/teal event chips, and holiday blocks aren't clickable.
