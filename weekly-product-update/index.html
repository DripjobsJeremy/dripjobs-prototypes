Proposal Builder

3 updates

Fixed Reopened Proposals Not Updating After a Stage Change in Command Center

Oct 26
What changed
Fixed a bug where changing a proposal's stage through the Command Center's Move workflow (for example, reopening a Rejected proposal to Draft) appeared to save but didn't actually update the proposal's status. Moving a proposal by drag-and-drop was not affected.
Why it matters
Reopening a rejected proposal through Command Center now reliably updates its status, the same as dragging it to a new stage.
Action needed
None

Fixed New Proposals Not Inheriting the Deal's Assigned Project Manager

Oct 26
What changed
Fixed a bug where creating a new proposal from a deal correctly defaulted the assigned Salesperson but left the Project Manager field blank, even when the deal had one assigned.
Why it matters
New proposals created from a deal now carry over both the Salesperson and Project Manager automatically, so you don't have to re-select the Project Manager by hand.
Action needed
None

Fixed Scheduled Proposal Sends Not Saving or Queuing in Some Cases

Oct 29
What changed
Fixed a bug that, for some accounts, prevented a proposal scheduled to send at a future date and time from saving or queuing. Clicking Send appeared to work but the proposal was never actually scheduled or sent.
Why it matters
Scheduling a proposal for a future send now reliably saves and sends at the time you selected.
Action needed
None

Appointments

Fixed Duplicate Calendar Entries After Scheduling an On-Site Estimate

Oct 26
What changed
Fixed a bug where the original booking-form request stayed visible on the calendar after an on-site estimate was scheduled from Command Center, creating a duplicate entry alongside the new appointment.
Why it matters
Your calendar now shows a single, accurate entry once an on-site estimate request has been scheduled.

None

Inbox

1 update

Fixed Email Unread Badge Showing Conversations You Can't Access

Oct 26
What changed
Fixed a bug where the unread badge on the Email menu could count conversations from deals you're not assigned to, even though your Inbox correctly showed them as not visible to you.
Why it matters
The unread count on the Email menu now always matches what's actually waiting in your Inbox.

None

Added Explanations to Contact Option Toggles

NewOct 26
What changed
Added info icons next to the Active Contact, Opt Out of Blasts/Newsletters, Opt Out of Drips, and Opt Out of SMS toggles on a contact's Options section. Hovering or tapping an icon explains what the toggle does.
Why it matters
It's now clear at a glance what each contact-level toggle controls, especially for the communication opt-out settings.

Invoicing

1 update

Fixed Request Payment's Custom % and Amount Fields Not Staying in Sync

Oct 29
What changed
Fixed a bug in the Request Payment modal where entering a dollar amount didn't update the Custom % field to match, and where a percentage entry rounded the calculated amount down instead of to the nearest cent.
Why it matters
The percentage and dollar amount in a payment request now always match, and amounts are rounded correctly.
Action needed
None

Production Rates

1 update

Added a 48-Character Limit to Substrate Client Labels

Oct 29
What changed
Limited the Client label field on a substrate to 48 characters going forward, with a live character counter, so new labels always display in full on customer-facing documents. Existing longer labels are unchanged and show a warning until edited back under the limit.
Why it matters
Client labels you add from now on will always show completely on proposals, invoices, and change orders instead of getting cut off.
Action needed
None
