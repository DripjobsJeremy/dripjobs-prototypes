**Period:** September 21 – September 25, 2026
**Release:** Sep 21 & Sep 23 deployments · 3 updates

## Integrations

### You Can Now Link More Than One Google Calendar (*New · Sep 21*)
**What changed:** You can now link more than one Google Calendar to your DripJobs account. All linked calendars show their events for reference, and you designate exactly one as the calendar DripJobs writes new events to, which you can change anytime without moving previously synced events.
**Why it matters:** You can see everything relevant to your schedule across multiple calendars while keeping DripJobs events organized in the one calendar you choose.
**Action needed:** None. Visit your calendar connection screen to link additional calendars if you'd like.

### Fixed Google Calendar Silently Falling Out of Sync When Reauthorization Was Needed (*Sep 23*)
**What changed:** Fixed a bug where a failed Google Calendar reauthorization could cause your calendar to quietly stop syncing instead of prompting you to reconnect.
**Why it matters:** If your Google Calendar connection ever needs reauthorization, you'll now get a clear reconnect prompt instead of your calendar silently falling out of sync.
**Action needed:** None. If you're ever prompted to reconnect Google Calendar, do so to keep your calendar syncing.
