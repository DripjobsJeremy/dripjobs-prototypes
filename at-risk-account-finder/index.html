At Risk Account Finder

CS Team · Super Admin View (Onboarding Reps included) · Find accounts at risk of churning by Health tier and signup date

Scope note: this is a reduced-scope tool, split from the broader HealthScore v2.1 draft (ticket 86bb90q17) per Tanner’s feedback: filter by Health tier and signup date, then give the rep enough contact info to reach out without impersonating the account. Everything else from the v2.1 draft (grid reduction, Account Details drawer, branch icons, copy-to-clipboard, etc.) is out of scope here and stays preserved on the HealthScore prototype.
Business Rule 9: Legacy accounts are excluded from this report entirely (not just hidden from an Account Type column). Nearly every Legacy account already clears the current “Healthy” threshold ($10K sales + A2P activated) regardless of actual engagement, so the Health Tier filter can’t reliably separate a genuinely at-risk Legacy account from a dormant one under today’s rule. Excluded until the Health tier definition is redefined in Activation Dashboard V1 Expansion.
