Proposals & Proposal Builder

6 updates

Category and Substrate Order Is Now Kept When Adding an Area

Nov 19
What changed
Fixed an issue where the order you pick categories and substrates in when adding an Interior or Exterior Area, for example Doors, Ceiling, Walls, was re-sorted after you clicked Next. The order you chose is now kept through Next and in the saved Area.
Why it matters
You no longer have to re-order items by hand while building a bid.
Action needed
None

Proposal Tax Rate Now Shows Three Decimals on Web and Mobile

Nov 12
What changed
In the proposal and template builders on web and mobile, the tax rate now displays to three decimals, for example 7.666% or 5.000%, including the Tax Rate row, the save message, the rate-difference banner, and the toggle options. Tax calculations, the Customer Portal, PDFs, and invoices are unchanged.
Why it matters
You can see the exact tax rate you are charging. The amounts charged were already correct.
Action needed
None

New: Description Only Option When Adding Multiple Items

Nov 9
What changed
The Add Multiple Items window on web and mobile now has a Description Only checkbox for each item, in proposals, packages, and proposal templates. It locks Qty, Price, and Optional for that row and cannot be combined with Optional. Description Only items add nothing to the proposal total and show no price on the PDF, Customer Portal, or invoice.
Why it matters
You can add a batch of description-only lines in one pass instead of editing each one afterward.
Action needed
None

"Edit Your Products and Services" Link Hidden From Non-Admin Users

Nov 5
What changed
In the Proposal Builder's line item window, the "Edit Your Products and Services" link is now hidden from non-admin users such as salespeople. They can still save a line item as a new product or service, and admins keep the link.
Why it matters
Your staff are no longer pointed to a settings page they cannot use.
Action needed
None

Proposal and Work Order PDF Text Now Matches the Invoice PDF Size

Nov 5
What changed
Table text on Proposal PDFs, including Change Order sections, and on Work Order PDFs is now larger to match the Invoice PDF. This covers line item tables, production rate area tables, and package line items. Descriptions that set their own size keep it, and the totals block, Proposal Summary, and address positions are unchanged.
Why it matters
Homeowners and crews can read line items and work orders more easily.
Action needed
None, long proposals may run a page or two longer.

Job Amounts Now Show the Proposal Price Everywhere

Nov 2
What changed
Job amounts on the contact page, deal window, Schedule page, calendar banners and visit pop-ups, Schedule Job dropdown, Proposals list, and Proposals Sent report now consistently show the proposal price. Multi-visit invoiced jobs no longer show doubled totals, and pending change orders are no longer counted on the Proposals list. The Job Pipeline and billing screens still use invoice totals.
Why it matters
The same job shows the same dollar amount everywhere, so recurring and multi-visit jobs are no longer overstated.
Action needed
None, expect Proposals Sent report values to change for jobs that were invoiced in visits.

Customer Portal & Invoicing

3 updates

Invoices No Longer Charge Tax on Change Order Areas When Production Rates Tax Is Off

Nov 23
What changed
Fixed an issue where Areas added by a Change Order were invoiced with the company tax rate even when Tax on Materials and Tax on Labor were turned off in Production Rates. The invoice now shows no tax on those Areas, and the invoice total matches the signed proposal plus change orders. Behavior with tax turned on, and tax on standalone line items, is unchanged.
Why it matters
Customers are no longer billed tax that was not on anything they signed.
Action needed
None, you may want to review past invoices that had change order Areas while Production Rates tax was off.

New: Text Message Option for Payment Receipts

Nov 2
What changed
For companies with Text Messaging, the Receive Payment window now lets you send the receipt by email, text, both, or neither, and Edit Payment has the same options, defaulting to No Receipt. The text is prefilled and editable, includes a link to the invoice, and is logged in the contact's communication history. Texts are not sent to contacts with no phone number or who opted out of SMS.
Why it matters
Customers get their payment confirmation on the same channel you use for proposals and invoices.
Action needed
None

"Offline Requested" Added to the Invoices Status Filter

Nov 2
What changed
The Status dropdown on the Invoices tab now includes "Offline Requested", so you can filter by every invoice status. Existing filters are unchanged.
Why it matters
You can quickly find invoices where the customer asked to pay offline.
Action needed
None

Pipeline & Mobile

2 updates

New: Business Records for Commercial Accounts

Nov 19
What changed
You can now create a Business as its own record, with a required Legal Name and optional DBA, email, phone, industry, and addresses, and associate several Contacts with it, one of them marked Primary. A new Businesses item in the Sales menu leads to a tabbed Business profile that brings together the activity tied to that business. You can create or choose a Business from New Lead, New Appointment, New Proposal, and Create Invoice, see Business icons on pipeline cards and calendar tiles, and turn on a Show Business toggle on the Contact List. A Business with open deals, invoices, or jobs can be archived but not deleted.
Why it matters
You can track commercial customers with several contacts under one business, with their deals and proposals in one place.
Action needed
None, create a Business for any commercial account you want to track this way.

"Generate Invoice" No Longer Offered Before a Proposal Is Accepted

Nov 5
What changed
"Generate Invoice" in Command Center > Invoices no longer appears for deals in the Sales Pipeline. It still appears for Jobs Pipeline deals that have no invoice, for example after an invoice was deleted. Accepting a proposal still creates the invoice as usual, and existing invoices and payments are untouched.
Why it matters
You can't invoice a proposal before it is accepted, which avoided status mismatches and Stripe payments not showing in DripJobs.
Action needed
None, invoices already generated early are not changed by this update.

Messaging & Notifications

2 updates

Text Messaging Setup Now Opens for Canadian-Dollar Accounts

Nov 12
What changed
Fixed an issue where, for accounts set to Canadian dollars, Text Messaging add-on setup refreshed the page instead of opening the configuration form after Continue and Complete setup. The form now opens, and a failed setup request shows an actionable error.
Why it matters
Canadian contractors can set up texting themselves.
Action needed
None, retry setup if you were stuck before.

Emojis in Email Blast Subject Lines Now Save Correctly

Nov 9
What changed
Fixed an issue where emojis typed or pasted into an email blast subject line were lost when saved and showed as "??". They now stay intact after saving, in the Live Preview, in test sends, and in recipients' inboxes.
Why it matters
Your seasonal promotion blasts no longer go out with broken "??" subject lines.
Action needed
Blasts or scheduled sends saved before this fix still contain "??", so re-enter the emojis in the subject.

Integrations

3 updates

Zapier "Find Most Recent Deal or Job" Action Updated to Help Move Deal Cards

Nov 23
What changed
The Zapier action "Find Most Recent Deal or Job" was updated to return the Deal or Job ID, plus a clearly labeled record type, so a Zap can find a deal by email and phone and then move its deal card.
Why it matters
Your automations can find a customer's deal and move it through the pipeline automatically.
Action needed
If you use this action in a Zap, re-test it and map the new ID fields if needed.

New: Zapier "Proposal Sent" Trigger

Nov 12
What changed
A new public Zapier trigger, "Proposal Sent", fires each time a proposal is sent or resent, including scheduled sends when they actually go out. It sends a snapshot of the proposal with totals, deal and customer details, line items, areas, substrates, and packages. It does not fire on manual deal-stage moves or on saves.
Why it matters
You can automate follow-ups and record keeping the moment a proposal goes out.
Action needed
Optional, create a Zap with the Proposal Sent trigger.

Zapier "Deal Stage Change" Trigger Test Now Works

Nov 2
What changed
Testing the Deal Stage Change trigger in Zapier now loads valid sample deals instead of failing with "Unable to pull deals". Accounts with no sample data get an empty result, and behind-the-scenes errors no longer expose technical details.
Why it matters
You can set up and test Zapier automations on deal stage changes without hitting a dead end.
Action needed
None

Settings

4 updates

New Production Rate Tutorial Video Series

Nov 19
What changed
Production Rate Guides in Settings > Production Rates now has an 8-part video series in place of the previous 4 videos, covering why to use Production Rates, setting up standard prep, pricing materials, labor, and total costs, a tour of the tabs, setting up substrates and areas, building a living room bid, and estimating exterior repaint labor and materials.
Why it matters
You get current, step-by-step training for setting up accurate bids.
Action needed
None, watch the videos from the Production Rate Guides section.

New: Full Logo Upload for Wide Logos

Nov 9
What changed
Company Settings > Brand now has an optional "Full Logo" upload for wide or horizontal logos, and the existing logo field is now labeled Logo Icon for your square logo. The Full Logo appears in proposals, invoices, work orders, the Customer Portal sidebar, and outbound emails, while the portal header box and watermark keep the Logo Icon. Brand Finder fills in both.
Why it matters
A wide logo now shows at a readable size instead of being squeezed into a square.
Action needed
Optional, upload a Full Logo if you have a wide logo. Once uploaded, emails, queued sends, and PDFs switch to it.

Expanded Starting Rates for Companies Adding Production Rates

Nov 9
What changed
Companies adding the Production Rates add-on for the first time now start with an expanded set of default base rates that merges with the existing defaults, including combined categories, a new Interior Closets category, many more substrates, and 30 products. Existing add-on accounts are not changed.
Why it matters
New users get realistic starting rates for their first proposals.
Action needed
None

New: Expired Payment Card Banner for Account Owners

Nov 5
What changed
Account owners now see a red banner when the primary card on their DripJobs subscription has expired, with an "Update Billing Details" button that opens Manage Billing. It shows on active and trial subscriptions and clears once a valid primary card is on file.
Why it matters
You get a warning before a failed charge interrupts your subscription.
Action needed
If your card has expired, update your billing details.

In QA
