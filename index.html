Rate Preset Templates
Clickable prototype for a new "Rate Preset Template" selector on Production Rates > Settings, letting an account start from a coaching-course or regional preset instead of entering rates from scratch. Manually editing a preset-linked substrate or product shows the required misconfiguration warning first. All preset values, substrate catalog values, and preset-suggested products are clearly-labeled placeholders pending final numbers; the Products catalog itself is real production data.
