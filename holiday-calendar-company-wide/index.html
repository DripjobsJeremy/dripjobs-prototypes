Holiday Calendar: Company-Wide Region, Per-User Display Toggle

Prototype for ticket 86bbv6zhh. Replaces the per-user region model shipped in 86bbc0rvr and its on-hold follow-up 86bbrazc3, superseded by this ticket and being closed (BR-9). Holiday Region (None / United States / Canada / United States & Canada) is now a single Company Settings value that a Company Admin manages for the whole company. The old per-user control is repurposed rather than replaced with something new: a "Holidays" entry now appears in each calendar's existing Event Type filter, checked by default, independently uncheckable per calendar, and split into separate US and Canada entries whenever both are enabled at once.

Signup and rollout defaults (BR-6, BR-10)
Holiday Region is derived automatically from the company's currency, set in Company Settings → Payments → Currency, the same mapping pattern Acorn Finance uses:
USD → United States
CAD → Canada
Any other currency → United States (fallback)
A USD company's Holiday Region would default to United States alone. This prototype starts on United States & Canada instead, so the independent US/Canada entries below (BR-2) are visible right away without changing the Region select first. The same currency mapping applies to every new signup going forward and was applied to every existing company at rollout, with the default visibility toggle enabled, regardless of any per-user region previously selected under 86bbc0rvr.
✏️
Correction to BR-2's assumption
The ticket's own flagged assumption (a single "Holidays" entry for whichever region is enabled) was wrong. When Region is United States & Canada, the Event Type filter shows two independent entries, US Holidays and CA Holidays, so a user can keep one country's holidays visible while hiding the other's. A single-country Region (United States or Canada alone) still gets just one "Holidays" entry, there's nothing to split in that case.
Before / after: the per-calendar control (BR-2, BR-9)
No new control is added to either calendar. The dedicated region select from the on-hold 86bbrazc3 prototype is gone; its job moves into the Event Type filter that already exists on the page.
