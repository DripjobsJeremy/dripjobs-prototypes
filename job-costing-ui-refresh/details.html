Dev notes for this screen

  • BR1 (section cards): status/contact, cost snapshot, profit margin/variance, Labor, Materials, Miscellaneous and Attachments are each a .settings-section card.
  • BR2 (floating labels): Add Subcontractor, Add/Edit Material and Add/Edit Miscellaneous modals use .settings-field floating labels; Amount uses the .settings-field-group "$" prefix and Date uses the icon-prefix group.
  • BR3 (buttons): every "+ Add" action is btn btn-sm btn-outline-primary (full-size in empty states), Save/Go are btn-primary, delete confirm is btn-danger.
  • BR5 / BR6: the Costing Status pill reuses the green status-tag family; all colors come from --st-color-* tokens scoped to #job-costing-details-page.
  • BR7 (Costing Status): two-way. The pill menu offers "Set as costing complete" while Active and "Set as costing active" while Complete. No Paused state, no lock.
  • BR8 (row Edit): the pencil on Materials and Miscellaneous rows opens the same modal prefilled ("Edit Material Cost" / "Edit Miscellaneous Cost"). Save updates that row in place and recalculates; Delete uses the shared "Are you sure?" confirm. A material that isn't in the catalog reopens in one-off mode with its name filled in.
  • Recalculation (AC): adding a labor pick, one-off subcontractor, employee time, material or misc cost (and editing or deleting a material or misc row) re-renders the donut, legend, profit margin, Estimated vs. Actual and every section header total from one revenue-minus-costs model.
  • BR10 (Add Employee Cost): the Clock In/Out step carries the same header as Bulk Date Selection: step label, "Name - $Rate" line and the read-only Employee field.
  • BR11 ("+ add time"): new hours are costed at the employee's flat rate with no overtime logic. Settings-aware overtime is out of scope (V2).
  • Copy fixes: "an one off" to "a one off" (Add Material Cost) and "Desription" to "Description" (Miscellaneous table header).
  • Not in scope, toast only: "+ add cost" and the chevrons on labor rows, the attachment card Edit button, and the Proposal / Invoice / Job Notes links.
  • Design system gaps (not blocking): variance badge orange, the blue selected-day color in date pickers, and the corner close "×" (only Material and Misc modals have it in production) aren't documented tokens or patterns yet.
  • Accessibility: semantic tables with scope headers, labelled icon buttons, Escape closes the top-most open layer, focus returns to the trigger on close.
  • Clean view (prototype-only): the "Hide callouts" button in the top bar hides the demo banner, orange BR/Flag chips and these dev notes on all 8 screens, for an unobstructed look at what an end user would see. It remembers your choice as you navigate between screens.
  • Sample data update: "John Lennon" renamed to "John Johnson" throughout (this table, the "+ Add Labor Costs" combobox, and the Employee Name filter on the Employee Timesheets Report). The job's crew is now 2 employees (Jesus Employee, Jerry Huffman, both at realistic ~$23-24/hr rates; Jerry's placeholder $90/hr combobox rate was also a leftover bug, fixed) plus 1 subcontractor (John Johnson), sized and priced like a typical large residential repaint, and the job is profitable at a 46% margin. Estimated vs. Actual now shows modest single-digit overages instead of the earlier 100%+ variances. The Dashboard's job card and summary strip were updated to match, since it's the same job shown on both screens.
  • Known gap, not fixed here: the Employee Timesheets Report still shows Jesus Employee's old hours (42:00 hrs / $989.00) for this job from before this data update; that report has its own independent sample dataset and wasn't in scope for this change. Flagging so it gets reconciled if the Reports pages get their own data pass.
