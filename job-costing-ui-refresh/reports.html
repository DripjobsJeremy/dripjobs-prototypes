Prototype: Job Costing Reports (index). One of 8 linked screens for ticket 86ba1ggxb. This is what the sidebar's Reports link opens, and the launch point for the Employee Timesheets and Employee Payroll reports. Sidebar and top bar are reference chrome, not restyled; the Job Costing sub-nav links all 8 screens. Orange chips map to the ticket's business rules, hover or tab to them. Sample data only.
