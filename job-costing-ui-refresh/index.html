Dev notes for this screen

  • BR1 (section cards): the profit-margin strip, donut breakdown and job list are each a .settings-section card.
  • BR2 (floating labels): Job Name, Status and Sort By use floating labels. The sentence-style "Showing jobs where [X] is within [Y]" filter stays inline, the confirmed BR2 exception.
  • BR3 (buttons): both "Go" buttons are btn btn-primary.
  • BR5 (tags): "Costing Active" is a status tag pill.
  • BR6 (tokens): colors come from --st-color-* tokens scoped to #job-costing-page; no legacy purple hex.
  • BR9 (Custom Dates): choosing "Custom Dates" reveals the start/end range with month/year dropdown calendars. This is existing production functionality carried into the refresh, not a new feature.
  • Layout fix (matches current production): the Revenue / Labor / Expenses / Gross Profit strip sits on one row with the items packed together (not spread edge to edge), matching Jeremy's screenshots of today's UI. It previously wrapped onto two lines with a large gap, then an interim fix over-spread the items with a big gap in the middle, then the metrics card was force-stretched to match the donut card's full height (which is taller, since it holds a chart plus a legend), leaving a large empty area below the single content row; all three corrected. The metrics card is now a compact bar sized to its own content, sitting beside the taller donut card rather than matching its height, per production. The metrics card takes the width it needs and the donut card holds a fixed width, rather than a fixed proportional split, so the row has room to fit on one line down to tablet width, where it wraps onto two lines instead of clipping.
  • Status filter (matches current production): corrected to the real three values, Costing Active, Ready for Costing, Costing Complete, confirmed from Jeremy's screenshot of the open dropdown. Added the matching .status-tag.ready / .status-tag.complete pill variants for future job rows in those statuses; the one sample job stays Costing Active, unchanged.
  • Sidebar (matches current production): added the Marketing (Activity, Blast, Drips) and Resources (DJ Academy, Automations) sections and the submenu chevrons on Proposals and Metrics, all reference chrome only.
  • Design system gaps (not blocking): donut segment colors and the blue selected-day color in the date picker are kept as production shows them but aren't documented tokens yet.
  • Accessibility: the donut has a text alternative via role="img" + aria-label, the job card is a single focusable link, and every control has a visible focus state.
  • Clean view (prototype-only): the "Hide callouts" button in the top bar hides the demo banner, orange BR/Flag chips and these dev notes on all 8 screens, for an unobstructed look at what an end user would see. It remembers your choice as you navigate between screens.
