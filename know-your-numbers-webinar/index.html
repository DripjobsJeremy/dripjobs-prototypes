Conversions & Volume

Closing Ratio

7 accepted ÷ 9 qualified = 78%
(same cohort, qualified in period)

Set Rate

1 booked ÷ 4 created = 25%
(same cohort, created in period)

Appointments

1 New + 3 Returning = 4 booked

Keep this in mind: Closing Ratio and Set Rate both follow one cohort through to its outcome — that's different from a simple period count. More on why that matters in a few slides.

“Why don't my numbers match?”
Brenda Templin's question
Closing Ratio

Follows one cohort

Tracks proposals qualified during the selected period, and reports what eventually happened to that exact group — even if the accept happens after the period ends.

Jobs Won

Counts period accepts

Counts every proposal accepted during the period, regardless of when it was originally sent or qualified. A different population of proposals.

Straight from the product:

“This tracks proposals that were qualified and/or accepted in the given period. Jobs Won may differ — it counts all accepted proposals regardless of when they were sent.”

dripjobs
?
“Why don't my numbers match?”
Looking deeper:
Closing Ratio only looks at proposals that were sent during the month you're viewing, and then asks what eventually happened to just those.
Jobs Won counts every acceptance that happened this month, even if you sent that proposal back in March.
So, if you had a slow proposal month but closed a bunch of old ones,
✅ Jobs Won goes up.
〜 while Closing Ratio for this month can look unrelated.
… and if you sold jobs through manual invoices instead of a sent proposal, Closing Ratio doesn't see those at all.
Different questions, same underlying data.
