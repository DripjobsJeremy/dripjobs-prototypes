Deep dive

Revenue & Wins

Sales

$26,310 Pipeline + $0 Manual = $26,310

Jobs Won

4 Signed + 3 No-Signature + 0 Manual = 7

Avg Job Size

$26,310 ÷ 7 jobs = $3,758.53

Use case: watch Sales and Jobs Won together for the month, then use Avg Job Size over time to spot pricing trends before they show up anywhere else.
Deep dive

Conversions & Volume

Closing Ratio

7 accepted ÷ 9 qualified = 78%
(same cohort, qualified in period)

Set Rate

1 booked ÷ 4 created = 25%
(same cohort, created in period)

Appointments

1 New + 3 Returning = 4 booked

Keep this in mind: Closing Ratio and Set Rate both follow one cohort through to its outcome — that's different from a simple period count. More on why that matters in a few slides.
